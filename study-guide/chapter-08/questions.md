**Q36.** A company's share price is ₹1,200 and its EPS is ₹48. What is the P/E, and the earnings yield?
A) P/E 25; earnings yield 4%  B) P/E 4%; earnings yield 25  C) P/E 25; earnings yield 25%  D) P/E 48; earnings yield 2%
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

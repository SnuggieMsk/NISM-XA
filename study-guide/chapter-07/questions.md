# Chapter 7: Introduction to Investments — 100 MCQ Question Bank

> Three tiers — 🟢 Easy (Recall), 🟡 Medium (Application), 🔴 Hard (Scenario & Discrimination). **Most questions here test contrasts**, especially MF vs PMS vs AIF. Remember the **25% negative marking**.

## 🟢 Tier 1 — Easy: Recall (Q1–Q35)

**Q1.** An equity shareholder has:
A) A contractual claim to fixed interest  B) A residual claim on the company's profits and assets  C) Priority over lenders in liquidation  D) A guaranteed dividend
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — Equity is **ownership**, carrying a **residual** claim — shareholders are paid only after all other claimants.
A) Describes debt. C) Reverses the priority — lenders rank **ahead** of shareholders. D) Dividends are discretionary, never guaranteed.
</details>

**Q2.** A bondholder is:
A) An owner of the company  B) A lender to the company  C) A director  D) A regulator
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — Debt means **lending**: a contractual right to interest and repayment of principal.
A) Describes equity. C) and D) are unrelated roles.
</details>

**Q3.** The minimum investment in a Portfolio Management Service is:
A) ₹5 lakh  B) ₹25 lakh  C) ₹50 lakh  D) ₹1 crore
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: C)** — SEBI prescribes a **₹50 lakh** minimum for PMS.
A) and B) are below the threshold. D) ₹1 crore is the **AIF** minimum — the classic confusion.
</details>

**Q4.** The minimum investment in an Alternative Investment Fund is generally:
A) ₹50 lakh  B) ₹1 crore  C) ₹10 lakh  D) ₹5 crore

**Correct: B)** — **₹1 crore**, reduced to ₹25 lakh for employees and directors of the AIF or its manager.
A) Is the PMS minimum. C) and D) are not the prescribed thresholds.
</details>

**Q5.** In a Portfolio Management Service, the securities are held:
A) In a pooled scheme  B) In the client's own name / demat account  C) By SEBI  D) By the stock exchange
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — PMS is **not pooled**; the client holds the securities directly. This is its defining structural difference from a mutual fund.
A) Describes a mutual fund or AIF. C) and D) never hold client securities.
</details>

**Q6.** The dividing line between investment grade and high yield debt is:
A) AAA  B) A−  C) BBB−  D) B+

**Correct: C)** — **BBB− (or Baa3)** and above is investment grade; below it is high yield.
A) AAA is the top rating, not the boundary. B) and D) sit on either side of the boundary but are not it.
</details>

**Q7.** Which asset class produces no cash flow?
A) Equity  B) Fixed income  C) Commodities  D) Real estate (let out)
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: C)** — Commodities pay **no interest, dividend or rent** — the entire return is price movement.
A) Equity can pay dividends. B) Debt pays interest. D) Let-out property generates rent.
</details>

**Q8.** Bond prices and interest rates move:
A) In the same direction  B) Inversely  C) Independently  D) Only upward
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — When rates rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons become less attractive, so their prices **fall**.
A) and C) are incorrect. D) Bond prices certainly fall as well as rise.
</details>

**Q9.** A REIT provides:
A) Direct ownership of a flat  B) Real estate exposure with the liquidity of a listed security  C) Guaranteed rental income  D) Commodity exposure
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — A **REIT** is a listed, SEBI-regulated trust holding rent-yielding property, solving real estate's illiquidity and large ticket size.
A) A REIT gives units, not a specific property. C) Distributions depend on rental performance. D) That would be a commodity ETF.
</details>

**Q10.** A structured product typically combines:
A) Two equities  B) A bond (or deposit) with a derivative  C) Two bonds  D) Gold and silver
<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct: B)** — Most of the money goes into a **zero-coupon bond** (for capital protection) and the remainder into **options** (for upside).
A), C) and D) do not describe structured products.
</details>

**Q11.** "Capital protection" in a structured product depends on:

A) SEBI regulates but guarantees nothing. C) The bond leg, not the market, provides the protection. D) The RBI has no role here.
</details>

**Q13.** Which of these is a mutual fund's structural feature?
